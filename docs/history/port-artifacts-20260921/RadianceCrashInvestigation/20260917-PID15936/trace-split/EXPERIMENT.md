Interpretation: neither trace alone prevents failure in these one-shot trials. Native-only crossed startup and failed later in item rendering, so startup and world/HUD outcomes must be reported separately. Earlier both-on successes were on the prior binary before bind-noop; do not infer that both-on is sufficient on this new binary without retesting. A higher aggregate instrumentation cost may alter timing, but no definitive causality established.
Next useful work: low-perturbation create/destroy/generation and frame-fence breadcrumbs tied to the captured failing draw; retain descriptor snapshots at draw boundaries to avoid losing their definition during rotation.
