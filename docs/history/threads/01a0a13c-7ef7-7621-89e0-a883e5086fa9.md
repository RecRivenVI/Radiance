## Durable engineering rules

- Diagnose and implement against a fixed source/mod version and the complete reachable lifecycle:
  initialization, resource creation, world and UI draw, off-screen targets, reload, dimension/world
  changes, and destruction. Reaching the menu or entering a world does not prove compatibility.
- Keep observation diagnostics, behavior-changing experiments, and production fixes in separate
  ledgers and artifacts. A wait, serialization, skipped draw, or extended lifetime that suppresses
  a crash is evidence, not automatically a correct fix.
- Remove investigation-only probes at the end of an investigation. Preserve normal error reporting,
  Vulkan result propagation, synchronization, ownership fixes, upstream diagnostics, and the real
  F3 GPU timing facility.
- Static analysis, compilation, automated tests, real-client runtime, deployment identity, and user
  visual acceptance are different evidence levels. None substitutes for a later level.
- Preserve the separate Radiance and MCVR upstream-shaped repositories. Do not revive the abandoned
  structural merge or Stonecutter-era reorganization from historical material.

## Historical snapshot facts worth retaining

- The 2026-09-14 audit fixed Radiance at `414d8e330a2fc6cb1e8630cc95f2302b2b97a0e8`
  and MCVR at `9905c81b1999f5845bf66d13501d371c16adf561`. It covered 457 changed paths
  and 1,249 diff units: 1,227 explained, 22 pending confirmation, and zero unmapped at that time.
- Sixteen selected reference-source entries were obtained or reused. Forge 1.20.1 and NeoForge
  1.21.1 had patched development sources; Fabric 1.21.4 had mapped/decompiled Minecraft plus
  separate Loader sources. These records did not claim that every runtime transform was materialized.
- The decision-review r2 later mapped the same historical 1,249 units with 17 remaining unknown
  units and grouped 52 compatibility records into 18 technical work packages. These numbers belong
  to that snapshot only.
- The compatibility checkpoint reported Java, native, shader, JNI, and packaging gates, but the
  first real A acceptance exposed a Mixin startup failure. The automated gates therefore remain
  engineering evidence, not product acceptance.
- Early real-client crashes occurred inside NVIDIA's OpenGL driver during GUI text/item draw paths.
  Validation also found real color-attachment synchronization defects, but those defects were not
  proved to be the cause of the native access violation.
- Later A/B/A experiments, outside the early summaries in this task, showed a strong repeatable
  relationship between keeping pipeline-layout ownership alive and avoiding the crash. The retained
  minimal workaround keeps the pipeline layout and descriptor-set layouts alive without retaining
  the complete descriptor table. Vulkan maintenance4 does not by itself prove that such post-create
  lifetime is required, so this remains a validated workaround rather than a fully explained driver
  root cause.

## Superseded or canceled material

Do not restore or act on the following from this task without a new review:

- The five-stage broad remediation plan was canceled before implementation and replaced by behavior
  decisions followed by bounded implementation work.
- Suggestions to remove the 50% name-tag background or disable the selected emissive hitbox/chunk
  presentation contradicted explicit product decisions and were rejected.
- Early statements that Veil, Sable, Flywheel, or public rendering were simply incomplete describe
  old snapshots. Later implementation changed those paths; current source and ledger entries govern.
- An early E-02 experiment retained an entire descriptor table. It was superseded by the smaller
  pipeline-layout ownership token and must not be reintroduced as the preferred design.
- Old resource-lifetime patches, validation-enabled stable runs, fixture passes, or a single
  non-crashing launch did not establish the native crash root cause.
- The task contains mutually superseding Prism/test-instance instructions. They were coordination
  decisions for specific experiments and are not a permanent project contract.
- Progress summaries produced after context compaction repeatedly reverted to older phases. Use the
  canonical files above and current Git state instead of those chat summaries.
