## Effective scope

The task started by resetting the two forks to upstream commits and became the initial NeoForge
1.21.1 port. It reached main menu and world loading, then expanded into Create, Aeronautics, Sable,
Flywheel fallback, Ponder, Veil, public rendering hooks, clouds/weather/world-border rendering,
first-person reflections, device-loss diagnostics, and text/RR experiments.

## Durable findings

- Preserve the separate Radiance and MCVR repositories and make port changes against their
  upstream-shaped source trees.
- Sable sublevel geometry initially never reached Radiance because the vanilla route had been
  canceled. The bridge needed section geometry, BLAS ownership, and sublevel transforms.
- Disabling Flywheel selected a fallback renderer; it was not a native Flywheel backend. Future
  claims must distinguish fallback compatibility from true instance-data integration.
- Entity, painting, item-frame, armor-stand, and plunger paths exposed coordinate/transform gaps.
  Painting relief/POM needed a dedicated render layer rather than a generic material assumption.
- One spring appearance issue was a stress-material mix, not ordinary alpha transparency. Material
  classification must follow the producer's meaning.
- Ponder and Veil could issue direct OpenGL operations after the main window became
  `GLFW_NO_API`; those calls required interception/translation rather than being allowed to hit the
  main window.
- NeoForge compatibility required the full event families later recorded in the broad integration
  extraction: hand/stages/highlights/section geometry/BER visibility/dimensions/fluids/custom
  particles/breaking overlays.
- A fence wait that returns device loss is an observation point, not automatically the cause.

## Superseded material

- Always-on device-fault probes and partial recovery work were later cleaned up or removed. Keep
  compact fault reporting, but do not restore an incomplete auto-recovery claim.
- One Nsight observation showed text intact before RR and broken afterward, leading to a temporary
  post-channel text route. Later source-first work changed the world-text design; the old route is
  not current authority.
- The task ended by requesting rollback of text and `waitForFences` experiments. Later tasks and
  current source determine the final state.
- Reset commits, transient branches, runtime hashes, and workspace paths are historical. The
  current `develop` baseline and ledgers supersede them.
